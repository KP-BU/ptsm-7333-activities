PTSM 7333 Faith and Sports Integration

Interactive activity previews for faculty review · 5 activities, one per week

How to review: open each activity and try it as a student would. Then choose Use it, Don't use it, or Request a change under it, here or at the bottom of the activity, and add a note if you like. When you're done, select Copy my choices and paste them into an email to keith_pace@baylor.edu. Your choices are saved in this browser as you go. These previews don't record anything about how you do in the activities.
Week 1

Sport in the Biblical Story

Drag and Drop

Students sort 11 items into 3 categories (Creation, Fall, Redemption), with feedback and a retry on anything misplaced.

Outcome: Determine a theological rationale for how to integrate faith and sports.

Open activity
Week 2

Seeing What Is There

Dialog Cards

Students turn 6 cards and work through each card's reflection prompts, with an optional note.

Outcome: Reflect on the unique cultural context of sports and how it forms and shapes Christians.

Open activity
Week 3

Five Virtues in Action

Drag and Drop

Students sort 10 items into 5 categories (Faith, Love, Discipline, Hope, Courage), with feedback and a retry on anything misplaced.

Outcome: Evaluate the FSI Retreat as a model for virtue formation through sports.

Open activity
Week 4

Virtues and Vices in a Runner's Season

Dropdown

Students complete 8 blanks by choosing from dropdown menus, then check and fix any that are wrong.

Outcome: Evaluate how the cultivation of Christian virtues affects athletic performance.

Open activity
Week 5

Faith/Sports Integration Planner

Guided Documentation

Faith/sports integration practice planner whose fields feed the Week 5 project proposal.

Outcome: Design a faith/sports integration practice.

Open activity
